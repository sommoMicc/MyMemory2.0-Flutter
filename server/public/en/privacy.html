Privacy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

SommoMemory is a memory matching game by Michele Tagliabue. You can play without signing up. The game creates a guest profile to keep your gems and collections; you can choose to enable recovery on other devices. It contains no advertising, payments or purchases with money. The service stores operational data to run the game and provide statistics accessible only to the operator, without advertising or cross-site tracking. Optional Google sign-in uses an authentication service with the data practices described below.

When you play on your device

Preferences, collections available offline, prepared games and credentials stay on your device. The game does not require access to contacts or the microphone. Solo games can be completed without a connection: the game keeps pending operations on your device and sends the board and move sequence to the service for verification when you return online. The app may immediately show pending gems and unlocks on your device; the service profile and your personal best are updated only after verification.

When you play via Bluetooth

To play without the internet, two nearby devices exchange the chosen name, moves and score directly via Bluetooth Low Energy. To claim the gem reward, the game sends the identifier of the prepared game and the sequence of revealed cards to the server when it ends. Your profile and recovery code are not shared with the other player. Bluetooth or nearby-device permission is required; on Android 11 and earlier, the system also requires location permission to discover BLE devices. The game does not obtain GPS coordinates for this feature. The approximate location Google may estimate from your IP address during sign-in is described in the relevant section below. You can revoke permissions in your device settings.

Gems, collections and an optional profile

The service stores a random profile identifier, an optional name, the avatar and colour chosen from the game catalogue, your gem balance, unlocked collections, a record of credits and unlocks, and the lowest number of attempts in solo games for each difficulty. Gems are not money and cannot be transferred. To award a reward only once, the service verifies online games and the move sequences of solo or prepared games completed offline. For solo games, it stores cryptographic fingerprints of the proof, not the board or move sequence. Prepared games expire after 30 days.

You can enable a recovery code to use the same profile on iPhone, Android and a browser, without creating a password or providing an email address. The server stores cryptographic fingerprints of game sessions and the recovery code. Anyone who has the code can access your profile: keep it safe and do not publish it. We cannot recover it for you. You can rotate it to invalidate the previous code; devices already connected stay connected until they sign out of the profile or you revoke them under Manage.

Optional sign-in with Apple or Google

On the Profile screen, you can link Apple or Google to sync gems, collections, customisations and your personal best in solo games. Local preferences stay on your device. The service stores the verified provider identifier and its link to your profile. We do not use email addresses to merge different profiles. The game code does not save email addresses, real names or photos returned by the provider on our server. Your chosen in-game name and avatar remain independent of that data. Other players do not receive your email address, provider identity or credentials.

Authentication is handled by Apple or Google in their own interfaces; SommoMemory does not receive their password. For Apple, the service stores encrypted tokens needed to verify and revoke authorisation. Credentials awaiting revocation after deletion are kept separate from the profile and deleted after revocation or, in any case, within 30 days. You can view connected devices and revoke their sessions. Some changes require you to verify your sign-in again.

Data used by the Google sign-in service

Google’s data processing is separate from the data stored by the game server. Google explains that its sign-in service may collect the user identifier and IP address, which is also used to estimate a general location and prevent fraud. You can read Google’s information about sign-in and data and Google’s privacy policy.

The privacy manifest of the Google Sign-In 9.2 SDK for iOS included in the app declares these categories and purposes:

  • Name, email address and phone number: sign-in functionality.
  • Approximate location: sign-in functionality, including security checks.
  • User identifier and other data categories: authentication service functionality and analytics.
  • Device identifier and other usage data: authentication service analytics.

The SDK manifest declares this data as linked to the user and not used for tracking. This is a declaration of the categories the SDK may process, not a statement that every field is collected at every sign-in. The game does not use this data for advertising or to analyse games. You can choose not to use Google and continue with a guest profile or another available method.

Deleting your profile

Your profile, balance and record are kept until you choose to delete them from the Profile screen or the Delete account page. Deletion revokes all sessions and the recovery code, removes the profile from the service and causes the loss of its gems and collections. For 31 days, we retain only fingerprints of deleted sessions, without a name or link to the profile, so that devices reconnecting can remove their local copy. A device that remains offline cannot receive the deletion; after this period, an invalid session requires recovery and the game conservatively keeps recoverable local data. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data removes the local copy, not the server profile. Multiplayer room information follows the separate retention period described below.

When you play online

The service at sommomemory.sommomi.cc receives your chosen name, moves, score and a random session identifier to connect you to the other person. The other person sees your name and the game. Do not share personal information in your name.

Inactive rooms are normally deleted within 24 hours of their last activity. An interrupted session can be resumed for a short time. The service stores only a cryptographic fingerprint of the room access token. Your IP address is needed for the connection and to limit abuse; the application does not add it to your profile or game record. The hosting infrastructure may produce technical request logs.

Private service statistics

The operator can access an administration area protected by a passkey to check active profiles, online games, lobbies and reward allocation. For each profile, we store the day and last contact with the service; repeated contacts are consolidated. Online history stores the game and room identifiers, mode, arena and collection, available timestamps, status, scores, attempts and outcome, linked to participating profiles. This operational data is retained for 90 days. Online history does not include boards, move sequences, IP addresses, email addresses, credentials or copies of player names. Displayed names come from the current profile; for guests without a profile, history shows a generic name. Solo games do not appear in online history: the service stores only the lowest number of attempts per difficulty in the profile and cryptographic fingerprints to prevent duplicate rewards. Deleting a profile also deletes its activity data and removes its link from history, where a generic participant remains. Aggregate counts may therefore change after deletion. We do not use this data for advertising or commercial profiling.

Your choices and contact

You can play offline with the collections available on your device. Leaving a room invalidates that game’s credential; signing out of a profile revokes that device’s session. Before replacing one profile with another, the game asks for confirmation: balances and collections from different profiles are not merged.

For questions, data requests or help, use the Support page. Do not include session tokens or personal information in public reports.
