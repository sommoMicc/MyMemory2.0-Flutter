Delete your SommoMemory account

You can delete your profile and its associated data without reinstalling the app. Deletion removes gems, unlocked collections, your name, customisations, sign-in methods and sessions. It is permanent.

Sign in with Apple or Google

Open the game in your browser. If necessary, sign in under Save and link, check that you have opened the correct profile, then choose Manage → Delete profile. You can also use an existing session.

Open your profile in the browser

This action deletes only your SommoMemory profile, not your Apple or Google account.

Use your recovery code

If you have kept your personal code, you can verify and delete the profile here. We first show which profile will be deleted.

The code starts with SMM. Do not share it with other people.

Delete this profile?

You will permanently lose its gems and collections. Other devices will lose access to the service. Offline devices do not receive the deletion immediately; the limits of local cleanup are described in the privacy policy.
