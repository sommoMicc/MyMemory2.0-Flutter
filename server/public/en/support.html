Support

How do I play online?

One person creates a room and shares its six-character code. The other joins with the same code from Android, iPhone, iPad or a browser. Both players must be connected to the internet.

How do turns work?

Reveal two cards. If they match, you score a point and keep playing; otherwise, the turn passes to the other person. Whoever finds the most pairs wins. Both players must accept a rematch.

The connection drops

The game stays paused for 90 seconds while the app tries to reconnect. Return to the same session without clearing app or browser data. If you leave the room, your opponent wins the current game.

Gems and new collections

A new profile receives 100 gems. Each verified completed game awards 20; leaving a game earns no reward. Animals, Farm and Flowers and plants are available in the starting arena; Paola, with its syllable cards, is available separately. As you advance through the arenas with crowns, you discover more collections, some free and others unlockable with gems. Verified unlocks stay in your profile. There are no payments or purchases with money. For multiplayer games, only the host needs to own the collection.

Offline play and rewards

Solo games can be played without a connection and without preparing them first. The game keeps completed-game operations on your device; pending gems and unlocks are immediately visible on the device. When you return online, the game sends the board and moves for verification, then syncs the operations and your personal best. Repeated syncing does not count operations twice. For local or Bluetooth games, prepare the game while online: the reward is verified when you reconnect. Do not clear local data until pending operations have synced.

The same profile on multiple devices

Open Profile, enable the recovery code and keep it safe. Import it on another device to recover your balance and collections; no email address or password is needed. You are asked to confirm before replacing the local profile. Different profiles are not merged. If your profile has no linked Apple or Google sign-in and you lose both the session and the code, it cannot be recovered.

Customise your player

In Profile, you can choose a name, avatar and colour without signing up. Avatars are part of the catalogue included in the game. Your personal best in solo games is saved in your profile and synced when the device connects; gems, collections and customisations sync with the same profile.

Apple and Google

When the buttons are available under Save and link, you can link your profile to Apple or Google. Linking a new sign-in keeps the current profile’s gems and collections. If that sign-in already belongs to another profile, the game shows a preview and asks for confirmation before switching. Balances are not added together. The provider’s real name and photo do not replace your in-game customisations. You can continue using the recovery code.

Rotating the code, signing out or deleting

Rotating the code invalidates the previous one without disconnecting other devices. Signing out revokes only the current session; you need the code or a linked provider to sign in again. Delete profile removes the balance, collections and all sessions: this action requires confirmation. Uninstalling the app does not delete the profile on the server.

You can also request deletion from the Delete SommoMemory account page without reinstalling the app. The page lets you use the recovery code or open your profile in the browser game. Signing out of a profile, unlinking a sign-in method and deleting the profile are different actions; to remove the last provider, you must keep another recovery method.

Report a problem

You can contact the developer through Michele Tagliabue’s GitHub profile. Include your device, operating system version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not publish credentials or personal data.
