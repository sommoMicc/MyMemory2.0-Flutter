# Arena journey references

Research: 6 November 2026. Original Flutter artwork; no reference artwork is
copied or bundled with the application.

- [Duolingo — The science behind the home screen redesign](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/)
  describes a guided path with a visible sequence of stops. Our application:
  connect the four arenas with a winding trail, distinguish reached and locked
  destinations, and show progress toward the next crown threshold.
- [Apple — Behind the Design: Duolingo](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=jhkvppla)
  discusses the learning path and the use of playful visual elements. Our
  application: give each arena its own illustrated landscape and use clear,
  consistent controls around that scenery.
- [King — Candy Crush Saga map and account controls](https://candycrush.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39931244875933-Where-can-I-find-my-settings-and-account-options)
  describes the Saga map as the game's starting point. Our application:
  expose arena exploration directly from the game setup, including individual
  play, while keeping collection selection and starting a game close at hand.

The journey is a visual treatment of the existing arena rules. Ranked crowns
still unlock arenas at 0, 100, 300, and 600; individual play earns gems and
unlocks collections without awarding competitive crowns.
