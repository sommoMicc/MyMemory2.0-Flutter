SommoMemory game feedback sounds

Original procedural synthesis created for this project on 5 November 2026.
No recordings, sound libraries, third-party samples or musical quotations used.
The reproducible generator is scripts/synthesize-feedback.py in the source repository; it is not bundled in the app. The resulting sounds and generator
may be used, modified and redistributed with this project without attribution.

Sprite: game_feedback.wav, mono signed PCM16, 22050 Hz, 3800 ms.
Silent unlock: 0-15 ms; flip: 40-95 ms; match: 1100-1300 ms;
mismatch: 2350-2510 ms; turn: 3550-3660 ms.
At least one second of silence separates the audible cues to absorb UI jank.
